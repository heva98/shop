we have to develop a shop management system (erp) which will cover the entire lifecycle of a tanzanian general retail shop (duka),
modelled on the pharmacy management system and sharing its design.
in mind we should take into consideration
1. a firm with more than one branch whereas each branch is a standalone entity
2. a firm with multiple branches with one branch acting as a main store/supplier to others after receiving enquiries and payments from others
NB. in the first type, the branches can request for products from the chat/enquire option whereby the other branches can see the enquiry and the ones capable can choose to help or not
payments: cash and mobile money/bank transfers (recorded with the transaction reference) are acceptable for now.
NB. integrated bank and mobile money transfer is not going to be integrated for now but the architecture is to be built in such a way to accept the future enhancements
as for notifications, for now whatsapp integration is not yet complete so the architecture is going to be built in such away it shall accept future intergration
but since the sms and email can be done. they are going to be set up now.
the information that is to be covered in the notification include:
1. the day's sales
2. the day's purchases
the above details are to be made daily.

what is different from the pharmacy system:
- no generic drugs, prescriptions, controlled substances or insurance claims
- products are grouped by category and carry a brand, a size and a barcode
- only perishable products (food, drinks, cosmetics) track expiry; other goods have no expiry date and need no batch number
- sales on credit to registered customers (madeni), with a credit limit per customer, repayments and a statement
- barcode scanning at the till and when receiving
- discounts per item and per sale, with a cashier limit above which a manager approves, and time-limited promotional prices

the features of the system will include the following:
A. if there is a main store, then it should.
1. show nearly stocked out items
2. show stocked out items
3. show expired items (perishables)
4. show nearly expired items (perishables)
5. display a list of all items(stock), number available, number expired, number eligible, number of batches. enable view or add batches, view the item in other branches
6. enable printing the items as specified
7. receiving new items.
-- on the receiving invoice, select the supplier from dropdown
-- enter invoice number
-- select the receiving date
--entry of items will be by scanning the barcode or searching, just typing letters would display the related items and they select the specific item and
---select its unit of measure from dropdown
--enter the order quantity
--enter the pack price
-- enter the batch number (optional)
-- enter the expiry date (perishables only)
--enter the batch quantity
8. be able to save an invoice as draft and be able to come back to it later and complete it only after saving would it be a received(completed invoice)
9. be able to search invoices by supplier, dates of receival, as well as being able to print the invoices
10. on receiving a request of items from the other branches, it should be able to issue items and reject the issue of items too as requested by other branches

there would be a setting option that will be present to all branches if there is no main store but if all other branches are dependent to a single main store then the settings
would only be available to the main store
1. enable registration of product categories by name, short name and description e.g Beverages
2. enable registration of products by name, category, brand, size, barcode and whether they expire e.g Sugar 1kg
3. enable registration of unit of measure by name, description and unit value or count of items e.g Crate of 24
4. have a ledger that would show operations (deduct or add) done by actions(ledger type) i.e Transfer In, Transfer Out, Stolen, Lost, Damaged, Expired, Spoiled, Wrong Entry, Sold, Discard, Consumption, Dispose
5. enable creation of re-order level quantity for commodities: this will be applied to all branches regardless as each branch no matter its dependency to the main store should have its own re-order level for its items
6. enable creation of supplier by their name, description, location and contacts (email and phone)
7. creation of price for products, for a single item as well as bigger packs (crate, dozen) and combined
8. creation of promotions: an offer price for a product for a period, for all branches or one branch
9. the largest discount a cashier may give without a manager

other branches would also have:
1. show nearly stocked out items
2. show stocked out items
3. show expired items
4. show nearly expired items
5. display a list of all items(stock), number available, number expired, number eligible, number of batches. enable view or add batches, view the item in other branches
6. enable printing the items as specified
7. receiving new items (as for the main store)
8. be able to save an invoice as draft and be able to come back to it later and complete it only after saving would it be a received(completed invoice)
9. be able to search invoices by supplier, dates of receival, as well as being able to print the invoices
10. on receiving a request of items from the other branches, it should be able to issue items and reject the issue of items too as requested by other branches
11. be able to request items from other branches by selecting a branch from the list, select the item and enter the quantity to request
12. be able to accept or reject items requested from the other branches

point of sale (all branches):
1. scan a barcode or search to add an item; sell in the stock unit or a bigger pack with its own price
2. give a discount on the sale; above the cashier limit a manager approves with their own login
3. choose a registered customer and put part or all of the sale on their account, within their credit limit
4. keep selling when the connection drops; sales are sent when it returns
5. open and close shifts with a cash count and an end-of-shift (Z) report that includes discounts and credit repayments

customers & credit (all branches):
1. register customers by name, phone, address; a manager sets each customer's credit limit
2. see what every customer owes and who is over their limit
3. take repayments (cash at the till counts in the drawer, or mobile money/bank with a reference)
4. print a customer's statement with the running balance
